The market, in three lines

About 70 verified live postings across eight passes. Around 20 are realistic for someone with zero years, no Dutch and no Nordic language. They sit in three places.

Brussels

Three paid EU traineeship windows open now

Council of the EU closes 15 September, Committee of the Regions 1 October, European Parliament 31 October. All paid, all English and French, all built for a first job. The Committee of the Regions is the best thematic fit found anywhere: it exists to represent cities and regions.

Spain and the Randstad

Her languages open Spain; English opens Dutch academia

Spain's second pass found a participation officer post in the Barcelona metro, two Big Four sustainability graduate programmes with zero-experience entry, and a social-inclusion role in Sant Adrià. The Netherlands offers two funded English-language PhDs at TU Delft and one placemaking internship that takes English speakers. Every Dutch permanent post requires Dutch.

Manchester

Many ads, one clean fit

Twelve live postings in her language, but HPI leave ends March 2028 and graduate salaries sit below the Skilled Worker threshold, so most graduate schemes decline a two-year visa on the sponsorship question. National Highways at GBP 39k clears the threshold and is the one UK row that works without a caveat.

Time-critical. Council of the EU closes 12:00 Brussels time on 15 September. Cordaid in The Hague closes 12 September but needs current enrolment, so one email first. Everything else in section 2 has two weeks or more.

8United Kingdom, and the visa arithmetic

The most live postings in a language she speaks, and the market most likely to filter her out before reading the CV. Twelve open postings were found. Against the visa arithmetic below, one is a clean fit, three are worth an email first, and the rest are graduate schemes that will most likely decline a two-year visa.

The 18-month problem, which decides the UK. HPI leave ends on 9 March 2028 and cannot be extended. Any role that runs past that date needs the employer to switch her to a Skilled Worker visa, and that needs three things at once: a sponsor licence (most small charities, community organisations and many planning consultancies do not hold one), an eligible occupation (town planning and sustainability roles qualify), and a salary at or above the Skilled Worker threshold. Under the rules in force from July 2025 that is GBP 41,700 general, or GBP 33,400 under the new-entrant discount for applicants under 26 or working towards a chartered qualification such as the RTPI C. Graduate planner and ESG analyst posts pay GBP 26,000 to 30,000. The employer therefore cannot keep her after March 2028 without a pay rise of 15 to 50 per cent, and the application form's "will you require sponsorship in future" question tells them so before anyone reads the CV. That, not the ad wording, is why UK graduate schemes decline two-year visa holders.

What survives it. Three kinds of UK role are still real. First, fixed-term work that ends by March 2028 anyway: an 18-month research post or a maternity cover needs no sponsorship and the question does not arise. Second, roles paying above GBP 33,400 at a licensed sponsor, where the switch is routine: public bodies (National Highways, combined authorities, Homes England, the Environment Agency) and the large consultancies all hold licences. Third, an employer small enough to decide on the person and honest enough to be told the date on day one, which is the Community Savers case. Everything else in the table below is an application that will most likely be filtered before a person sees it, and the time is better spent on Brussels and Spain.

10The four checks that decide an application

Every row above carries one or more of these. None is a reason not to apply. Each is a sentence to put in the cover letter or an email to send first.

Enrolment clauses Dutch and Spanish internships often require being "currently enrolled" or a live university convenio. A graduate cannot take them as written. One email to HR asking whether a July 2025 graduate qualifies costs nothing and sometimes works; a postgraduate certificate course or a UPF alumni scheme can restore convenio eligibility if a specific beca is worth it.
The visa end date, UK only HPI ends 9 March 2028 and cannot be extended. A switch to Skilled Worker needs a licensed sponsor and a salary at or above the threshold (GBP 33,400 new entrant, GBP 41,700 general, July 2025 rules, verify). Below that salary, or at an unlicensed employer, any permanent UK role is an 18-month role. Say the date in the first paragraph; the unanswered question is what gets filtered.
Experience bars "1 to 2 years" at EY and "al menys 1 any" at Pere Tarrés are soft; "3 años mínimo" at ECODES and "two years relevant" at the JPO are hard. For the soft ones, the dissertation fieldwork, the Living Lab and the committee year are the evidence. Never round zero up to one in the CV itself.
Degree titles and accreditation Spanish posts asking for arquitecte or enginyer are hard filters. UK ads saying RTPI-accredited "preferred" are soft: the Groningen MSc is a two-year spatial planning master's at an EU planning faculty, and she can pursue RTPI Licentiate membership through the experience route. Say exactly that.

Honesty rule, restated because it applies to every row: state the zero years plainly, lead with the dissertation method and the Living Lab deliverables, never cite the Estapé-Mir internship or the GeoPromotion conference. A reviewer who reads "recent graduate, two participatory research projects, three working languages" believes it. A reviewer who reads a padded CV believes nothing on it.
